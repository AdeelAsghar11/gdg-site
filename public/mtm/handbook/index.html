03 / Arrive ready

A little preparation goes a long way.

  • Bring your laptop, charger, and the development tools you plan to use.
  • Keep your student identification and payment reference available for the registration desk.
  • Arrive for check-in between 9:00 and 10:00 AM at New Building, B26 New Hall.
  • Solo participants are welcome. Teams may have no more than 3 members.

Campus entry requirements and internet availability will be confirmed by the organizers.

04 / Build, submit, present

Problem statement to solution.

One problem statement will be revealed during the 10:00–10:30 AM opening. Use AI tools to build a working solution to that problem. Building runs from 10:30 AM to 2:00 PM, followed by project submission and lunch from 2:00–2:30 PM. Jamming and evaluation run from 2:30–4:30 PM.

AI tools are part of the challenge. The detailed problem brief, submission format, evaluation criteria, prize distribution, and event rules will be shared by the organizing team.

The three judges will be revealed soon.
